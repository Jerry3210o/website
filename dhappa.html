How ordering works

Three ways to order

Order online through our checkout page, call us and read out the item numbers, or print the mail-in form and send it with a check. All three are explained plainly on How to Order.

Shipping is $7.95 anywhere in the 50 states, and free on orders over $75. Most orders leave us in 1–2 business days.

What we will never do

We do not run countdown timers, “only 2 left” warnings, or pre-checked add-ons. We do not show star ratings until verified customers post them. We do not show crossed-out “was” prices, because this store has no price history to compare against.

If anything here is unclear, call a person: (555) 010-0147.
